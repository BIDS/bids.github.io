BioJupyter Team Notes

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

Who we are

The BioJupyter project at the Berkeley Institute for Data Science (BIDS), University of California, Berkeley, operates the BioJupyter Team Notes application ("the application"). The application controls access to the internal notes of the BioJupyter team.

Data that we get from Google

When you sign in, Google sends the application this data about your Google account:

  • your name;
  • your email address;
  • your profile picture URL;
  • your Google account identifier.

The application requests only the openid, email and profile scopes. It does not get access to your Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, contacts or other Google data.

How we use this data

We use this data only to:

  • verify your identity;
  • check that you are a member of the BioJupyter team;
  • show your name in the application while you are signed in.

We do not use this data for other purposes.

How we keep this data

After you sign in, the application keeps your identity in a session cookie. The cookie is removed when you sign out or when the session expires.

Our server logs can record your email address and the time of each access. We use these logs only for security and to fix problems. We delete log data when we no longer need it for these purposes.

We do not keep a separate database of Google user data.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your Google user data with third parties. We do not use it for advertising. We do not use it to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models. We disclose data only when the law requires it.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app by the application obeys the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Cookies

The application uses only the cookies that are necessary for sign-in and for your session. It does not use tracking or analytics cookies.

Your choices

You can remove the access of the application to your Google account at any time on your Google Account permissions page. You can ask us to delete data about you. Use the contact address below.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and change the effective date.

Contact

Send questions about this policy to stefanv@berkeley.edu.
