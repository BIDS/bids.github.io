About BioJupyter

BioJupyter is a modular, open platform for biomedical discovery. It is built on Project Jupyter. The project supports collaboration across biomedical research, from basic biology to clinical work.

BioJupyter is a project of the Berkeley Institute for Data Science (BIDS) at UC Berkeley. It is part of the Bakar Computational Biomedicine Initiative. The initial focus is on labs at UC Berkeley and UCSF. Read the project announcement for more information.

About this application

The BioJupyter team keeps internal notes for the project. This application controls access to these notes. You sign in with your Google account. The application then checks your email address against a list of team members. Only team members can read the notes.

The application asks Google only for your name, email address and profile picture. It does not get access to your Gmail, Drive, Calendar or other Google data. The privacy policy gives the full details.

This application is for members of the BioJupyter team. It is not a public service.
